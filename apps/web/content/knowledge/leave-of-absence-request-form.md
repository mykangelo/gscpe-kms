Section 14. Tenure, Leave of Absence and Honorable Dismissal

14.1 Tenure. A student must finish the requirements of a college course within one and one-half (1 ½) times the normal length of the prescribed residence for the course; otherwise he or she shall not be readmitted into the University. If a student is officially given permission for a leave of absence (maximum of one academic year only), the leave is not counted in the computation of tenure. If a student takes more than a year of leave, he or she is admitted as a returning student upon evaluation and recommendation of the Chairperson, Academic Head, Dean or Director.

14.2 Leave of Absence. A student who plans a leave of absence longer than one semester but not more than one academic year files a letter of intent with the College Dean or Branch Director stating the reasons for the leave, and the Registrar's Office is given a copy. If the leave exceeds one academic year, the student loses the status of student-in-residence. A student who withdraws from the University without a formal leave of absence must apply for readmission as a returning student.
