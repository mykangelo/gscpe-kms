Request for Correction of Name and/or Date of Birth. Office of the University Registrar, Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sta. Mesa, Manila. Form no. PUP-RCNB-5-UNRO-025, Rev. 2, February 18, 2025.

The student writes to the Assistant University Registrar (Jaime Y. Gonzales) asking for a correction of a name or date of birth that was entered wrongly in the school record. The letter states the student's name, the program or level, the semester and school year of admission, the item to correct, what it says now, what it should say, and the reason for the correction.

Supporting documents to attach (3 copies):
1. Letter addressed to the University Registrar through the Office of the University Registrar explaining the circumstances that led to the erroneous entry of data in the school record.
2. Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate.
3. Parent's Affidavit or Affidavit of Discrepancy.
4. Joint Affidavit of two (2) Disinterested Persons.
5. Corrected copy of F137-A (High School Permanent Record).
6. Proof of Payment: PhP 150.

Student number: ____________________
Item to correct (name or date of birth): ____________________
Current entry: ____________________
Correct entry: ____________________
Reason for the correction: ____________________

The Records Analyst certifies the request against the records on file, and the Assistant University Registrar approves it and certifies the correction.
