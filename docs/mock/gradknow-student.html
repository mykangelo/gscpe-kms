Knowledge that stays with the program

Answers about graduate study often sit in Facebook posts, chat threads and individual memory. GRADKnow gathers them into one organized system maintained by the Graduate School of Computer Engineering (GSCpE).

  1. CollectProgram details, forms and approved documents go into one repository.
  2. OrganizeContent is sorted by type, program and student stage.
  3. AnswerThe AI assistant retrieves from official content and points to the source.
  4. MaintainAuthorized staff keep entries current each school year.
